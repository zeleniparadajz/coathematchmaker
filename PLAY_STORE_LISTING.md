## Full description

COA The Matchmaker is a tennis community app for local clubs, leagues and players who want an easier way to organize matches, tournaments and rankings.

Create your player profile, find available tennis partners, send match challenges, agree on time and location, upload match photos and keep your results in one place. Competitive matches can count toward rankings, while friendly matches stay useful for scheduling without affecting points.

For clubs and league admins, COA makes it simple to create tournaments, manage players, generate brackets, approve results and keep a clean ranking table.

Key features:

- Player profiles with photo, club, country, sport and match status
- Match challenges with confirmation flow
- Friendly and competitive matches
- Tennis tournament creation and player management
- Rankings sorted by points and wins
- Match and tournament galleries
- Direct messages between players
- Admin tools for tournament and match management

COA is built for tennis players who want less waiting, less confusion and more time on court.
